Frequently asked questions

Find advice about getting around Kazakhstan, when to go, and what to pack. Search opens matching answers automatically.

Citizens of many countries can stay visa-free for a limited period. Rules change, so check the official e-visa and migration pages before you book.

May to September suits most tours. Mangystau is best in spring and autumn, and the mountains around Almaty are great for skiing from December.

Prices in this student website are example figures only. No actual transport, guides, accommodation or other services are sold or included. A real provider would supply a detailed written itinerary and inclusions.

This is an educational travel-discovery prototype, so it does not accept bookings or payments and there is nothing to cancel. Always read a real operator’s cancellation policy before paying.

The sample itineraries have no fixed group size. If you book through a real provider, ask about group size and accessibility before choosing a tour.

No guides are contracted through this demo website. For a real trip, confirm your preferred guide language directly with the tour operator.

The local currency is the tenge (KZT). Cards work in cities, but bring some cash for markets and small villages on the tour.

Comfortable hiking shoes, a warm jacket, sunscreen and a water bottle. The weather in the mountains can change quickly, even in summer.
